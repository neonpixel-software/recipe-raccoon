# App Store listing

The store text for Recipe Raccoon (PLAN 12.2) in all five languages, ready to paste into App Store
Connect: the app → each language → App Information (name, subtitle) and the version's page
(promotional text, description, keywords). iOS and macOS take the same text; enter it for both
platforms' versions.

The words match the app: the capture options are called what the add-recipe screen calls them
(Type It, Paste a Link) in each language, and Spanish, German and Dutch use the informal "you"
like the app, French the formal one. Native speakers checked the translations before the first
submission (2026-11-03), like the app's strings (#65, #156).

Weight is in the English listing only, like the weight screenshot (`screenshots.md`): the density
data names ingredients in English, so a recipe written in another language gets no weights.

Apple's limits: subtitle 30 characters, promotional text 170, description 4,000, keywords 100
bytes, so an accented letter counts as two (Apple's "Platform version information" reference),
separated by commas without spaces. Keywords shouldn't repeat the name or subtitle, which are
already searched. Promotional text can change at any time without a new version; the rest only
with a version. The French subtitle is exactly 30 characters, so recount it after any change.

When the app changes, check the listing against it, like the privacy answers
(`app-store-connect.md`): every feature named here must work as described.

```
Recipe Raccoon keeps your recipes clean: the ingredients and the steps, without the ads, pop-ups and life story that come before the first cup of flour.

ADD A RECIPE YOUR WAY
• Paste a Link: paste the address of a recipe's web page, and Apple Intelligence pulls out the title, ingredients, equipment and steps.

Nothing is saved before you've reviewed it, so you can fix anything first.

• Ingredients, equipment and numbered steps, laid out clearly on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Paste a Link and Type It need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other devices, you add recipes by filling them in.
```
